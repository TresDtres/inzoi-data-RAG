# Vehicle

Vehicles that appear in the game. Sort by clicking a header, search by name, or filter by category.

How to read this

`Type` comes from the data table — bicycle, motorcycle, car and ship.

Rows marked `Unreleased` exist in the data but cannot be bought in game. All eight ships fall here: they have a Blueprint but no purchase level and no icon.
