### 07. Data reference

- [VehicleTemplate](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/docs/modkit-docs/wiki/structguide/vehicletemplate) - the shared template audio and dimensions belong to.
- [FB1VehicleAssetDescription](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/docs/modkit-docs/wiki/structguide/struct/fb1vehicleassetdescription) - the vehicle's physical dimensions.
- [ObjectBP](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/docs/modkit-docs/wiki/structguide/objectbp) - where the bounding box is stored.
