Export your inZOI mod using the "Create Local Mod Package" feature to test or share it locally.

---

## 01. Local Mod Package

Click on **Create Local Mod Package**.

![Local Export Step 1](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/media/Local/Local_01.jpg)

---

## 02. Choose save location

Set the path to `This PC > Documents > inZOI > Mods`

![](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/media/Local/Local_02.jpg)

After you choose the folder, a notice appears before packaging starts. Click **OK** to continue.

To skip the notice from now on, select **Don't show this again** before clicking **OK**.

To bring it back, turn on **Show packaging notice** at the bottom of the **Package MOD** drop-down on the toolbar.

---

## 03. Confirm success

If you see a message in the bottom right corner of the screen saying “Packaging successful,” the process is complete.

![Local Export Step 3](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/media/Local/Local_03.jpg)

---

## 04. Verify In-Game

Launch the game and check Build Studio to see if the created furniture has been applied correctly.

![Local Export Step 4](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/media/Local/Local_04.jpg)

## 05. Packaging failed!

If you see the Packaging failed! message, it most likely means that one or more required assets are missing.

```
Make sure that at least one of the following is properly assigned: Mesh, Material, or Texture.
```

If any of these assets are missing, the packaging process cannot be completed successfully. Please double-check in the Content Editor to ensure that each element is correctly linked.

![Local Export Step 5](https://mod-docs.playinzoi.com/media/Local/Local_05.jpg)
