Mitigates single-file convoy behavior.`LaneSearchIteration``integer`Number of lane search iterations used to find a valid spawn lane for a vehicle.`MaxInstancesNearby``integer`Maximum number of vehicle instances allowed in the nearby AOI area before blocking a new spawn.`MaximumSpawnRange``number`Maximum distance from the lane start or end beyond which spawning is disallowed.`MinimumSpawnRange``number`Minimum distance from the lane start or end below which spawning is disallowed.`NearbySearchSteps``integer`Number of AOI steps used to search for nearby vehicles during spawn checks.`SpawnExcludeCameraAngleDegrees``number`Full cone angle (degrees) around camera forward. A spawn point inside this cone is refused outright - there is no fallback, so a tick where every candidate is on screen spawns nothing. An empty road is better than a car appearing out of nothing: for a viewer, and for anything trained on the footage, a vehicle that was not in the previous frame is a broken fact, while missing traffic is merely quiet. The cone is the whole test - it is deliberately not paired with a distance band the way B2SpawnerHelper::IsVisibleSLOD0Spawner pairs it for crowds. That band is MediumDistOut (6000 uu by default), the distance crowd characters stop being rendered; vehicles are drawn far beyond it. Pairing them would leave the outer part of the spawn ring judged "not visible". 180 = the entire front hemisphere, matching UB1CharacterSpawnControllerDataAsset's SpawnExcludeCameraAngleDegrees. Kept identical on purpose: crowds and traffic answer the same question, and two different answers would show up as pedestrians and cars popping in at different distances in the same shot. Lowering it does NOT make spawning safer - it opens a wedge in front of the camera where cars may appear. Only lower it if the spawn ring sits beyond the distance vehicles are drawn at, where the cone stops mattering.`SpawnTrialCount``integer`Maximum number of spawn attempts per vehicle when trying to place it in the world.`VisibleDespawnRangeScale``number`Hard removal distance, as a multiple of MaximumSpawnRange, for a traffic vehicle that is still being rendered. Range-based removal is skipped while a vehicle is on screen, otherwise a car receding down a straight road is destroyed in plain sight - the same broken fact as a pop-in, played backwards. But "still rendered" can stay true indefinitely down a long straight, so this bounds it: past this distance the vehicle is removed no matter what. It has to be comfortably larger than 1.0, or the guard buys nothing. |
