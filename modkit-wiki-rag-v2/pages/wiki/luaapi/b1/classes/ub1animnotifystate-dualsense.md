# Overview

- Range-based DualSense effect AnimNotifyState. Calls PlayDualSense on NotifyBegin and StopActiveDualSense on NotifyEnd. Suited for loop/Persist presets. For one-shot presets, use UB1AnimNotify_DualSense.
