| NameTypeDescription`AutoPickupInteractionBagId``string`AutoPickup (holding an object during an aggro chase) - a single location-agnostic Bag branches bag/world pickup via SequenceStartInfos. The location-agnostic InteractionBag used for auto-pickup. NAME_None means auto-pickup is not queued.`AutoPickupWorldSearchRadiusCm``number`Search radius (cm) for auto-pickup world candidates. Used for the surrounding world search of ObjectId/ObjectTag/AnyObject.`ChaseInteractionBagId``string`InteractionBag auto-queued at chase start. NAME_None disables auto-queueing (chase will not fire).`DecayPerMinute``integer`Amount subtracted from the total per minute (game time, oldest-first).`FarDecayMultiplier``number`Per-minute decay multiplier above FarDistance. The farther, the faster the decay.`FarDistance``number`At or above this distance (cm), FarDecayMultiplier is applied. Linearly interpolated between NearDistance and FarDistance.`MaxAggro``integer`Numbers — common to all aggro Max aggro total per (Source, Target) pair. Sum of all Reason buckets is trimmed (oldest-first) to stay at or below this.`MaxTargetsPerSource``integer`System guard Cap on the number of concurrent Target aggro entries one Source may hold.`NearDecayMultiplier``number`Per-minute decay multiplier below NearDistance. 1 = keep the existing flat decay.`NearDistance``number`Distance-based decay acceleration - always applied (no option). Decides the decay multiplier from the Source-Target distance. At or below this distance (cm), NearDecayMultiplier is applied.`OffsetDistance``number`Default arrival distance used when TableRow.OffsetDistanceOverride is negative (WalkTo f1 OffsetDistance).`OriginLeashDecayMultiplier``number`Multiplier applied (multiplicatively) on top of the distance-based multiplier when leaving the origin.`OriginLeashDistance``number`Extra decay triggers when the Source moves at least this distance (cm) from the aggro origin (initial capture location). Applied only to Reasons with bUseOriginLeashDecay on.`PathRefreshIntervalGameMinutes``number`Chase - common re-path interval during chase (game minutes). After applying the PathFollower wait bypass (ClearWaitRequestPath), even a 1-minute interval has little jitter impact.`TargetMoveThresholdCm``number`Re-path is requested only when the Target has moved at least this distance from the last refresh location (throttling). 200 cm recommended so short jitters do not re-path every time. |
