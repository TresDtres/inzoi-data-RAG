# Description

Changes the relationship between Self and Target from spouse to a normal relationship. Disconnects spouses in the family tree, clears the family relationship, and updates the main relationship to Divorce. Resets Romance to MinRelationshipStat, Friendship to 0, and Business to 0. Adds ExSpouse flags to both characters.
