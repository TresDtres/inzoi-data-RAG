# Description

Grants PaidVacation to all employees in the same Business career as the BaseObject. Members without vacation get PaidVacation applied for the next work day. Members already on vacation get +1 to their remaining vacation days.

# Parameters

| ParameterDescriptionBaseObjectAny member of the target Business career. |
| --- |
| BaseObjectAny member of the target Business career. |
