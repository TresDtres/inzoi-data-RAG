Finds the restaurant table currently assigned to a Self or Target party customer, including PendingGuide. When Self is an on-duty employee in a customer-hosted Coop, the Coop host is used as the customer.

# Parameters

| ParameterDescriptionInContextSelf/Target is a party customer, or Self is the employee participating in a customer-hosted Coop.InFilterBaseObject=Self or Target, S1/S2/ConditionId=None. |
