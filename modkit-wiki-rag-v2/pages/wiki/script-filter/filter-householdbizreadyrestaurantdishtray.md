Finds a Ready restaurant dish tray this server can serve, regardless of the order it was cooked in.

# Parameters

| ParameterDescriptionInContextSelf must be an on-site employee on duty or the manager of the operating restaurant. A manually picked Ready tray is accepted when it is unclaimed or claimed by Self and is held by Self or placed on business furniture. Without a pick, the earliest created servable tray is chosen.InFilterBaseObject=Self, S1/S2/ConditionId=None. |
