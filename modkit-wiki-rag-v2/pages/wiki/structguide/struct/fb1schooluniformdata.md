School uniform storage. The school-wide uniform designated per gender at the school's main-hall mannequin (outfit/accessories only; no hair/makeup). When a uniform is designated, each enrolled student's CategorizedStyles[School] is overwritten with it (their personal School look is lost).

| PropertyTypeDescriptionMetadataUniformByGenderTMapDesignated uniform outfit per gender. A missing key means that gender is unset (students keep their personal School outfit). |
