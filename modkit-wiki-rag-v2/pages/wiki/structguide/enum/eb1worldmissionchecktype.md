## 🎛️ Enum: EB1WorldMissionCheckType

Specifies which scope the mission condition applies to.

- **None**: No scope is selected.
- **Character**: The check applies to a specific character.
- **Family**: The check applies to the family as a unit.
- **World**: The check applies to the entire simulation world.
