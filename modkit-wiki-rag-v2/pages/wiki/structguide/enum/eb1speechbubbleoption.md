## 🎛️ Enum: EB1SpeechBubbleOption

Specifies different visual styles for speech bubbles.

- **Basic**: Displays a basic speech bubble.
- **Icon**: Shows an icon instead of text.
- **Talking**: Indicates the character is actively talking.
