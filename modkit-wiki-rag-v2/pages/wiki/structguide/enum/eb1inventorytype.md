## 🎛️ Enum: EB1InventoryType

Defines different types of inventories used in the game.

- **Invalid**: Invalid or uninitialized inventory.
- **Pocket**: Personal inventory (e.g., carried by character).
- **Site**: Inventory linked to a site.
- **Package**: Moving box or package-based inventory.
