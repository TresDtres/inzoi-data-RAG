## 🗣️ Enum: EB1ChatbotType

Specifies types of chatbot interactions used in various gameplay contexts.

- **Invalid**: Represents an undefined or invalid chatbot type.
- **FreeChat**: Open-ended conversation without constraints.
- **VideoChat**: Chat interaction tied to a video or cutscene.
- **Tutorial**: Guided conversation for tutorials or instructions.
