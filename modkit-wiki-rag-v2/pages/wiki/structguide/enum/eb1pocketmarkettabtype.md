## 🎛️ Enum: EB1PocketMarketTabType

Tabs available in the Pocket Market interface.

- **Shopping**: View items available for purchase.
- **HomeService**: Order home services.
- **Sell**: Sell items.
- **Orders**: View current or past orders.
