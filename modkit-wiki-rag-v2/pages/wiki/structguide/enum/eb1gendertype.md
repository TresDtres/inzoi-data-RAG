## 🎛️ Enum: EB1GenderType

Represents gender classification for characters.

- **Invalid**: Invalid or uninitialized gender.
- **Default**: Use default gender setting (e.g., context-defined).
- **Female**: Female gender.
- **Male**: Male gender.
