## 🎬 Enum: EB1ActingType

Defines the type of acting behavior applied in scenarios or interactions.

- **None** (`0`): No acting behavior is applied.
- **Essential**: A core or one-time acting action.
- **Loop**: Repeated acting, typically used for continuous behaviors.
