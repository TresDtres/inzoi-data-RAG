## 💠 Enum: EB1EmotionTendency

Represents the general tendency or nature of an emotion.

- **Invalid**: Undefined emotion tendency.
- **Natural**: Neutral or baseline emotion.
- **Positive**: Pleasant or uplifting emotion.
- **Negative**: Unpleasant or stressful emotion.
- **Max**: Internal limit marker.
