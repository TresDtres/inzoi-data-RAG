## 🎛️ Enum: EB1Anim_Direction

Defines possible directional movement states for animations.

- **Forward**: Forward direction.
- **Left**: Left direction.
- **Right**: Right direction.
- **Backward_L**: Backward-left direction.
- **Backward_R**: Backward-right direction.
