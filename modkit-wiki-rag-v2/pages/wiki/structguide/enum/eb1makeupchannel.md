## 🎨 Enum: EB1MakeupChannel

Represents the individual color channels in a texture used for makeup or mask application.

- **R** (`0`): Red channel.
- **G** (`1`): Green channel.
- **B** (`2`): Blue channel.
- **A** (`3`): Alpha channel.
