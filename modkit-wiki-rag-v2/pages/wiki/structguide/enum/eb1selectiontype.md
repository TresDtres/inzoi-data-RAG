## 🎛️ Enum: EB1SelectionType

Represents categorized options used throughout simulation and logic systems.

- **Normal**: Represents a normal selection mode.
- **Remote**: Indicates a remotely controlled selection.
- **Door**: Selection pertains to a door object.
