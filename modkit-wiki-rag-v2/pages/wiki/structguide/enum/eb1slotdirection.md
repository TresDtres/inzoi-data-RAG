## 🎛️ Enum: EB1SlotDirection

Defines the slot direction this override applies to (e.g., Left, Right), relative to the direction the character is facing when interacting with the slot.

- **None**: Indicates that no specific value is selected.
- **Left**: The slot faces left.
- **Right**: The slot faces right.
