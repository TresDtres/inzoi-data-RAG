## 🎛️ Enum: EB1WeatherType

Represents the current weather condition in the game world.

- **None**: No weather condition is currently set.
- **Sun**: Sunny weather.
- **Cloud**: Cloudy but dry weather.
- **Rain**: Rainy conditions.
- **Snow**: Snowfall is occurring.
