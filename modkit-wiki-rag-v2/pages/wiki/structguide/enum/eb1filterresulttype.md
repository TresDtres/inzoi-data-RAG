## 🎛️ Enum: EB1FilterResultType

Indicates the result type from a filtering operation.

- **Invalid**: Invalid or uninitialized filter result.
- **NearTarget**: Closest matching target.
- **FarTarget**: Farthest matching target.
- **TargetList**: A list of all matching targets.
