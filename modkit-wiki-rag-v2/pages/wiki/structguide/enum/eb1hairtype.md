## 🎛️ Enum: EB1HairType

Represents different hair styles for characters.

- **None**: No specific hairstyle.
- **Straight**: Straight hair.
- **Wavy**: Wavy hair.
- **Curly**: Curly hair.
- **Bald**: No hair.
- **MAX**: Internal limit marker.
