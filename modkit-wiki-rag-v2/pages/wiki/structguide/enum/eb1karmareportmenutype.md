## 🎛️ Enum: EB1KarmaReportMenuType

Defines menu categories within the Karma Report UI.

- **Invalid**: No specific menu.
- **KarmaDashboard**: Karma status dashboard.
- **ZoIList**: List of ZoI character.
- **GhostList**: List of ghosts.
- **HermanNotice**: Notices from Herman.
