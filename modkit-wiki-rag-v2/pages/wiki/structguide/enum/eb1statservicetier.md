## 🎛️ Enum: EB1StatServiceTier

This tier influences autonomous behavior by prioritizing the selection of InteractionBags based on the tier tire. It is linked to the Tier column in the InteractionBag sheet.

- **Invalid**: Indicates an invalid or uninitialized value.
- **Full**: Full tier.
- **Limited**: Limited tier.
