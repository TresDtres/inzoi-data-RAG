## 🎛️ Enum: EB1SeasonType

Represents categorized options used throughout simulation and logic systems.

- **None**: Indicates that no specific value is selected.
- **Spring**: Represents the spring season.
- **Summer**: Represents the summer season.
- **Fall**: Represents the fall season.
- **Winter**: Represents the winter season.
