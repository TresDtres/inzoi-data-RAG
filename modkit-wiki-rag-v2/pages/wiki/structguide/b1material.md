## 🧩 Struct: FB1MaterialTableRow

Defines a material resource entry for use in appearance or environment customization.

### Properties

- **Id** (`FName`): Unique identifier for the material.
- **MaterialInterface** (`TSoftObjectPtr<UMaterialInterface>`): Material asset referenced by this entry.
