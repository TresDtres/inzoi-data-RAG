## 🧩 Struct: FB1LightObjectTableRow

Defines configuration for light-emitting objects in the environment.

### Properties

- **Id** (`FName`): Unique identifier for the light object.
- **LightComponentNames** (`TArray<FName>`): Names of light components associated with this object.
