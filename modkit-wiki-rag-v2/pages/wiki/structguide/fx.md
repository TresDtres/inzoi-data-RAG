## 🧩 Struct: FB1FxTableRow

Defines a visual effects entry used in gameplay or interaction events.

### Properties

- **Id** (`FName`): Unique identifier for this FX entry.
- **FxObject** (`TSoftClassPtr<UObject>`): FX class object (e.g., Niagara, Particle).
