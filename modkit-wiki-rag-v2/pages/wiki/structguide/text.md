## 🧩 Struct: FB1TextTableRow

Defines a single localized text entry used for tooltips, instructions, or messages.

### Properties

- **Id** (`FName`): Unique identifier for the text entry.
- **Text** (`FString`): Localized string mapped to this entry. *(StringTableKey)*
