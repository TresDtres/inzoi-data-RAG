## 🧩 Struct: FB1MissionTableRow

Defines a mission or quest entry, including its condition requirements.

### Properties

- **Id** (`FName`): Unique identifier for the mission.
- **Conditions** (`TArray<FB1MissionConditionData>`): List of condition checks that determine mission success.
