# What are we supposed to do with mini gold bar from treasure hunt ?

**Sintoma:** I played with one save the treasure hunt on Wednesday at the resort. The lack of guidance is bad. What are we supposed to do once we found those golden bars ? The kiosk doesn’t take them, nor an ATM

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I want to correct my statement regarding the option of exchanging gold bars at the resort, but couldn’t because it was greyed out. I just went back to the coin exchange kiosk and if you hover over the option - it states that you need 10 gold bars minimum to do the exchange. So that explains why I couldn’t do it since I currently have 8. So to answer your question properly, you can exchange your gold bars at the resort by using the coin exchange kiosk. **Edited
