**Sintoma:** Hardware and Operating System Specifications (OS version, CPU, GPU, RAM, storage…): Windows 11, 12th Gen Intel(R) Core™ i7-12700H (2.30 GHz) I installed the DLC. I started the game with “Continue Game,” using my last save. Two of my families were no longer in their homes, but in the family container. The lots are empty, but the family description has the address where they lived, so it doesn’t allow me to move them to the lot. I solved the problem with one of the families by moving them to a new, empty family, and then I was able to put it back in the lot. The second family has two babies, and I can’t do this.
