# Weird Triangular Shading Still Appearing after Update

**Sintoma:** 98 KB Automatically appears in both build and live mode, in old and new saves. zip (20.1 MB) Triangular shading appearing on certain items - cabinets, hood ranges, counters, and fridges. Oddly, only some fridges are affected (shown below) and corner cabinets/counter are also unaffected. I cannot find this issue on any other items, and the items still work as intended, they just look weird. I was hoping this bug would fix itself with the recent update but it’s still appearing, hence the re-post. I just bought the game and have been really enjoying it but this has made me stop playing because it’s so distracting. Someone please help!! Screenshot 2026-09-16 165914 1276×809 232 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Update for any who might need this at some point: I fixed it by changing the Nanite setting to Off! In previous posts there had been people saying to keep it on to resolve rendering issues but in this case the opposite worked for me lol
