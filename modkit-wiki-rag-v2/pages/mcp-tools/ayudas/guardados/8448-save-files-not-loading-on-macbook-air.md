# Save files not loading on MacBook Air

**Sintoma:** These mods have since been removed from Shadow PC VM. Serenity Wave Hair by Enai Seashell Body Chain by Apaloosa Male Hair 01 by TheKuntswollen Elza_LoveandDeepSpace_hair1_D584E7CB by elza47598 Luna Grace Hair by Enai Midnight Silk Hair by Enai Heart Leather Choker by PlayersWonderland Niven Hair 06 - Hyunsoo Hair Specifications & Operating System : OS Version: Sequoia 15.5, CPU: GPU: Apple M4, RAM: MacBook Air - configurable to 24GB or 32GB, Storage: 256GB N/A Although there is no error message on my Mac, my saved files don’t appear in the lobby. On Shadow PC, they do show and are selectable. MacBook Air Specifications: Hardware Overview 834×406 24.3 KB Apple M4 Chip 834×470 21.1 KB MacBook

**Causa:** Mac permission access settings

**Solucion:**

1. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders and check if inZOI has been properly added
2. If inZOI is not listed, uninstall inZOI via Steam and restart your Mac
3. After restarting your Mac, reinstall inZOI via Steam
