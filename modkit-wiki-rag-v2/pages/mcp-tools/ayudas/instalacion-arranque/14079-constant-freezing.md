# Constant Freezing

**Sintoma:** While playing in Build Mode or Live Mode my game will freeze within 30 minutes of gameplay. It will not unfreeze but it will not crash either. I have to manually close the game via steam and restart it if I want to play longer. There is no pattern to the freezes and I have attempted various fixes such as the following; Verifying game files via steam Turning on and off autosave Turning off Smart-Zoi Removing mods/canvas creations Removing old shaders and restarting Allocating 60,000MB to virtual files It has nothing to do with my GPU usage, I notice I am often around 40-60% of GPU usage whenever I freeze. There are no problems with other games and I have not had this problem before the update

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:**

1. Turn autosave to 30 minutes or off (Temporarily) Turn off Smart Zoi (I kept this off permanently) Clear Cache in settings Create a new world Once you’re in your new world, you can adjust the graphics/simulation settings
2. I noticed as I changed my autosave settings the time between freezing would change
