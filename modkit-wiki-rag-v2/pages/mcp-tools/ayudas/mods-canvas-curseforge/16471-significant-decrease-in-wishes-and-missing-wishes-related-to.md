# Significant decrease in Wishes and missing Wishes related to low Needs

**Sintoma:** Significant decrease in Wishes and missing Wishes related to low Needs Latest version available Steam – Windows Currently not using any mods Hardware and Operating System Specifications: Windows 11 Home 64-bit Launch the game using the latest available version. Play normally with one or more Zoi characters. Observe the Wishes that appear during gameplay. Let some of the Zoi’s Needs decrease to a low level. Observe the Wishes that appear when the Needs are low. Expected Result: Wishes should appear regularly and consistently according to the Zoi’s current situation and Needs. When one or more Needs become low, related Wishes should appear to encourage the Zoi to take care of those Needs. Actu

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi everyone ( @here ), thank you for clarifying your concern. Wishes related to Needs were intentionally removed. As the variety of Wishes has expanded, we wanted Wishes to focus more on content-related experiences and activities, rather than prompting players to fulfill Needs that Zois can generally take care of on their own. This change itself is not related to Zois having difficulty managing their Needs. If you notice that your Zoi is not properly taking care of their Needs, please submit it as a separate bug report with the relevant details so we can look into it further. Thank you for your understanding and for taking the time to share your feedback!
