# The game crashes after entering the world

**Sintoma:** When I try to play, the game crashes. After updating the game, I decided to return to the game and play on a new map. At first, I had mods, created a new game, entered the world, selected a location, and after about 30 seconds, the game crashed without any errors. I removed the mods, created a new game, entered the world, and after about 30 seconds, the game crashed again. I reinstalled the game, created a new game, entered the world, and after about 30 seconds, the game crashed again. I don’t know what the problem might be. I tried different graphics settings, but it didn’t work. I also checked the game files, but it didn’t help

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I solved the problem. I went to the mod folder and saw the mods there, I deleted them and everything worked. BlueClient\Content\Paks~mods I had installed these mods after the game was released, and they were probably causing conflicts
