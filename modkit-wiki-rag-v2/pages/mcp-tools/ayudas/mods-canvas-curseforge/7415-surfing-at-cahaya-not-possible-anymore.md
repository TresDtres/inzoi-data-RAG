# Surfing at Cahaya not possible anymore

**Sintoma:** There are two of these surfboards at Cahaya for surfing in the ocean, but they are not funcional for this anymore, just for the practising on land. We’ve been able to surf the after the update but it’s not possible anymore. (old save, no mods, fully updated) grafik 1920×1080 385 KB Screenshot from November 9th tumblr_9971cbaff2847632f1bbbf9d8abea1c6_17d5f109_2048 1920×1080 618 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** @LennyOgg this is completely crazy…. and it took me a moment to understand. I was really playing in a new save file but still had an old one from September. So I just tried it out and loaded it up. There I had a Zoi with Level 4 in Fitness and one with Level 1 Fitness. Both were at home, so quite far away from the beach. I just moved over to the beach with my mouse leaving them at home and as expected I was able to select surfing for her (Level 4) but not for him (Level 1). Then I ordered her to go to the beach and perform the action. She did it but only once and then she was also not able to select the “surf” option anymore. So I thought it is broken for the old saves. But I just spent some more time in this save and found out that next morning it was available again, first not but then it was. So I started to investigate and spent an entire day with her at the beach. What happens is: before 9am you are unable to select the “surf” option: Screenshot 2025-11-29 154816 1920×800 401 KB But after 9 am it is there: Screenshot 2025-11-29 154828 1920×800 384 KB And also the surfing works without any issue: Screenshot 2025-11-29 153925 1920×800 475 KB Then in the evening it ist the same
