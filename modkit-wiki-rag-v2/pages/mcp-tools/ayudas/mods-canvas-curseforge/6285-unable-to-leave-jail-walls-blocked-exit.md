# Unable to leave jail! Walls blocked exit.

**Sintoma:** Screenshot 2025-10-28 124049 1909×872 305 KB 10/28/2025. Because the walls are blocking the exit, the police officers can’t feed my character. When my character jail time was over, he still couldn’t leave bc the exit is blocked. It says “You can’t move here!” I never installed any mods or anything

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** If I recall correctly, you have to use an interaction on your Zoi to leave jail once their jail time is over. It should show when clicking your Zoi sometime after the time is up. If that doesn’t work, just use the “Emergency Rescue” button in Psycat’s Guide. Your Zoi will respawn outside the jail. And the police officers don’t feed your character. There should be a slot on the wall somewhere that the Zoi has to click during certain times of the day to get food. But remember that water also fills up a Zois hunger, so you can have them drink water to fill them up. Welcome to the Forums, btw!
