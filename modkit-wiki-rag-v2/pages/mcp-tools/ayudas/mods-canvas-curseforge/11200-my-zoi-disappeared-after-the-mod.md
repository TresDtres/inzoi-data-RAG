# my Zoi disappeared after the mod

**Sintoma:** After installling the mode, my Zoi disappeared. The game sees the character in the game but he is invisible he cant go somewhere. I removed the mod, but still be the same result… for some reason i cant add the last safe here…

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Yesterday I had a similar situation. A Zoi from one of my Zoi families suddenly became invisible. When I clicked on his avatar, the camera zoomed to the invisible Zoi, who couldn’t be controlled. After switching to another household and then switching back to the invisible Zoi, he was no longer invisible
