# Half of the mods in Mod Kit cann’t be activated due to an error

**Sintoma:** I understand that the problem will probably be solved faster if I write to the mod creators directly. But what if I can’t find the creator of some mods either on the inZOI forum or in the Canvas? Mod Kit Error 1920×1080 215 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:**

1. It appears that the mods you mentioned are currently incompatible after the recent update, and unfortunately they cannot be applied at this time
2. Regarding how to contact the mod creators, please find the steps below: Go to the Mod Browser (C/F) and navigate to “Installed Mods” Select the mod you want and open its detail page Click on “Comments” to leave a message
