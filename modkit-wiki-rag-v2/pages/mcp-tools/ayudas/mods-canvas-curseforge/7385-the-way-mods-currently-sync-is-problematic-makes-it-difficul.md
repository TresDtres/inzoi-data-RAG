# The way mods currently sync is problematic, makes it difficult to troubleshoot mods I have created

**Sintoma:** In 0.3.0, mods would sync very easily. The game would recognize changes made to jsons even while in the lobby and this made it very easy to troubleshoot mods I created. Now in 0.4.0 and beyond, it has become very difficult to get mods and mod updates to sync. I have to constantly delete the inZOI folder in Documents to ‘refresh’ the mods and sometimes even that won’t work. This is terrible for mod creators. Please think of rolling back to the system of how the game recognized mods in 0.3.0

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** This happened to me as well when 0.4.0 hit. Took me a long time to figure it out and it was a pain. It also happened to someone I know and thankfully I was able to tell them to delete the folder and re-sub to the mods… Hopefully it gets fixed
