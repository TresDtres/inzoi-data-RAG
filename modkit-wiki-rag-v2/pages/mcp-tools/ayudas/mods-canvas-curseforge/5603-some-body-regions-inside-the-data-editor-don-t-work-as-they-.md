# Some body regions inside the Data Editor don’t work as they should

**Sintoma:** Open modkit, create your cc, and when you asign the regions in the “body regions” category, there are a few regions that don’t work. Instead of being selected when you select them, a different region pops up. Inside the Data Editor, when you go to Garment____Mesh and assign the appropriate body regions to your garment, some of the body regions have a glitch that cause a different region than the one you select to appear. I’ve made a video where you can see this happening to two of the regions, but it happens to as total of 4. which are the following: Upper Body Short glitches to Back Upper. Back Body glitches to Upper Body. Boots glitches to Socks. Boots Long glitches to Socks Long

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi @itsmechiie7 , the issue you reported has been addressed in our October update . Please refer to the patch notes for detailed information. If you encounter the same issue again, don’t hesitate to report it
