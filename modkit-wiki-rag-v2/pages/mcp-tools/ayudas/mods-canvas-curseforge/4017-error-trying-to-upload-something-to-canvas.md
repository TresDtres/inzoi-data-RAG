# Error trying to upload something to Canvas

**Sintoma:** Windows 11 24H2 x64 KB5063878 26100.4946 AMD Ryzen 9 7950X RTX 4060 8GB 32GB 6000MT/s SSD NVMe 1’8T Failed to upload Canvas item. [E04] Dependences not found When I try to upload something to Canvas, I get these messages. It doesn’t matter if it’s a Zoi or an outfit, it won’t let me upload anything, giving me those two errors, but I can download things. The problem isn’t any textures or images, since if I try to upload a basic Zoi with an accessory removed, it won’t let me either. It’s a bit frustrating not being able to share my creations with the community. Thank you so much for your time. Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CWFKY_qql6g

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Starwing: there are three variants with leopard-print on the skirt, and all other variants have a waistband that cannot be customized. I can’t upload any of the first three variants that have leopard-print skirt (customized or default). I am able to upload the variants that have waistband we can’t customize. I had one pattern that went well with one of those waistbands, so I uploaded that to Canvas, but most of those waistbands are colours that don’t appeal to me and aren’t likely to match anything I would create. I prefer the leopard-print skirts that can be customized with one texture or colour that’s applied to the waistband and the skirt. I really want to be able to share the unique styles I’ve made for these outfits. Hi there! I had this happen to me before and what I did to finally be able to upload was to save only the face preset, get out of CAZ, come back into CAZ and then put the face preset into a new body and redoing body shape, outfits, etc. Maybe it won’t work for you but I wanted to share in case it helps
