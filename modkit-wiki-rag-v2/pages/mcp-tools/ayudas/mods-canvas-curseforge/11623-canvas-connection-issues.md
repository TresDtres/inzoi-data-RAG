# Canvas connection issues

**Sintoma:** image 894×667 151 KB image 1491×907 104 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Welcome! Perhaps the firewall on your machine is blocking the network access. Ensure that from your default browser, outside the game, you can access and connect to Canvas. In your firewall, the following files should have access to the network. (It’s probably EpicWebHelper.exe in your case that may not have network access) <SteamLibraryPath>\steamapps\common\inZOI\BlueClient\Binaries\Win64\inZOI-Win64-Shipping.exe <SteamLibraryPath>\steamapps\common\inZOI\Engine\Binaries\Win64\EpicWebHelper.exe C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\bin\cef\cef.win64\steamwebhelper.exe Since you have installed the game from Steam, I’m assuming that the Steam exe and Steam service have network access
