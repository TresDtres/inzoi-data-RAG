# Option to restock cereal shelf does not appear

**Sintoma:** For the convenience store job, the option to “restock shelf” does not appear on Cereal shelf. You can only organize shelf. This means you can never complete the task for it at the job. Playing at the AZ Convenience store in Dowon. The Zoi can autonomously restock the shelf but if you click it, the option doesn’t show

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi @justanotherzoi , “restock shelf” only becomes available after a certain number of purchases have been made at the cereal display. Please try again after making some purchases
