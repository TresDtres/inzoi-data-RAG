# Business – How To Remove Spoiled Food?

**Sintoma:** When running a business such as the Bakery or Cafe, how do you remove spoiled food from the display cases? I must be missing something obvious but once it’s in the display case I can’t seem to activate any of the usual interactions to clean up the spoiled food. Only way I’ve found is to go into Build Mode and delete the display case entirely then install a new one. There must be a better way I’m missing. Thanks for any help!

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I was able to click on the item spoiled in the display, kind of gotta move the mouse over it to get its info pop up, click and wash the dishes. All spoiled items were removed instantly. Hope this helps
