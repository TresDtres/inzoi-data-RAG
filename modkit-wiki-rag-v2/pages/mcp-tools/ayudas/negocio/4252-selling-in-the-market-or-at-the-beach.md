# Selling in the market or at the beach

**Sintoma:** I’m sure this will sound stupid, but how do you sell outside of your property? When I go to the market and try to buy a mat or stall, it takes me back to the zoi’s property. I need to sell as the woman in the vendor shop disappeared

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** You can edit community lots by clicking on the ‘Edit Public Property’ icon next to the ‘Build Mode’ icon but you have to be at the lot with your active Zoi. grafik 1920×1080 231 KB
