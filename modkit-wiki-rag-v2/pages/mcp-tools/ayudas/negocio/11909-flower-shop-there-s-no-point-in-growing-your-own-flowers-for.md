# Flower shop. There’s no point in growing your own flowers for sale.

**Sintoma:** I want to ask why the cost of bouquets made with flowers grown by Zoi is the same as the cost of the restock option? A bouquet costs 3 meows, a basket costs 5 meows in both cases. I think it defeats the purpose of growing your own flowers for sale. We’ve already spent money on seeds and labor to grow the flowers. I think assembling the arrangement should be free

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** @LeeLoo That’s exactly what I mean. I also don’t buy them from pocket market. But in the moment when you start crafting the mentioned flower price - which is the same as if you would buy it from pocket market - is charged. Not only the 3 or 5 Mew is charged but the flower price on top. The only difference is that it is not shown as cost before you start crafting directly in the table menu. But you should see the charge for flowers you don’t have directly above your Zois head in red numbers which are deducted from your budget. I can take a screenshot later when in game. The difference between crafting in business and at home is that in business it does not force you to buy the flowers beforehand. Probably to avoid overhead as also your employees might craft flower arrangements and you cannot give the flowers to them in a meaningful way at the moment. While when crafting at home the game forces you to have the flowers in your inventory, so you need to buy them. That is how I noticed that the price charged for the flowers above the Zois head in business is exactly the same which you would pay at pocket market when you make the arrangements at home. Does this not happen in your game?
