# Can I sell objects, potted plants, or furniture in my business?

**Sintoma:** I know the flower shop is coming next week, but I was wondering if I can already sell, for example, household items, potted plants or furniture. I’m not familiar with the business, but if the business options are “bar”, “club”, “bakery”, then I could still offer other things for sale? Could someone who has experienced this tell me?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I don’t think this is possible (at least yet). If you put down e.g. crafted items or grown crops there is no option to sell these while in the shop. Regarding furniture there is also no possibility to put these on sale.
