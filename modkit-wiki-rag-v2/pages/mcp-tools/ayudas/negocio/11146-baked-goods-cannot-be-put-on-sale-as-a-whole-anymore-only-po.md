# Baked goods cannot be put on sale as a whole anymore (only portioned) - after Birthday Update

**Sintoma:** select restock items for sale and baked goods - Zoi always will portion the cake now and not put the whole cake up for sale 20260329-113545.zip (23.0 MB) Before the Birthday update it was possible to put baked goods on sale as a whole or to portion these. Now after the patch even if you select the option “Place Market Display” the Zoi will portion the baked goods. Before it only happened if you selected the “Portion and Display” Option. Does this mean that we cannot sell whole cakes anymore? I don’t think this is intended thus why I am posting a bug. Please clarify. See screenshot: even with the option marked in red the Zoi puts the baked goods on sale per portion like seen in the market sta

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Same here. Zois automatically slice everything before putting it on display. The only way I managed to avoid it was going in to build mode and putting the whole cake in the display from there. I hope they fix that interaction so it can go back to work normally as it did!
