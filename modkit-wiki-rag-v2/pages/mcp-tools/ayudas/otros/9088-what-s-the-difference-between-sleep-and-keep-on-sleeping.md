# What’s the difference between ‘sleep’ and ‘keep on sleeping’?

**Sintoma:** My Zoi did not sleep before, but there is a ‘keep on sleeping’ option when I click on the bed. No matter if I chose ‘sleep’ or ‘keep on sleeping’, he just lies down an sleeps. What might be the difference - or the reason why there are two options? image 1920×1080 307 KB The only difference I can find is the text in the action bar image 1920×1080 312 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Based on the answers from the Dev on a similar question Zoi Sleeping are subjected to the artificial routines that will wake them up before 11pm or after 8am Keep Sleeping bypass that code, and your zoi keeps sleeping
