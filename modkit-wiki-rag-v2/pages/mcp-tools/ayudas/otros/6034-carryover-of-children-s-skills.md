# Carryover of children’s skills?

**Sintoma:** Is there any carryover of children’s skills (e.g. Creativity) when they turn into adolescents? If not, what is the point of them? When one of my Zois aged up from a child into an adolescent, all of the new adult skills seemed to be reset to 0

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I had a child Zoi just recently who maxed out 4 of the currently 6 child skills. All 4 gave the relevant skills at level 5 when aging into an adolescent. Social brought Rhetoric and Charm on level 5. Creativity as you mentioned @Liessahl + media production also at level 5. Motor skill turned into fitness at level 5. Responsibility gave Housework at 5. So even not knowing for sure I assume that the new child skill for swimming will turn into swimming and / or snorkeling skill at level 5 if maxed out. And the curiosity child skill then will likely turn into gathering, fishing and maybe also crafting. Can someone confirm? Also it seems that the toddler traits also carry over. I had only 2 of them maxed out, but for those 2 the chid Zoi started already at level 3
