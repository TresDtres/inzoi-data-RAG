# Door not closing behind zoi accidentally causes visual glitches

**Sintoma:** Place any usable object close to door. Door wont close after the zoi enters the room, and therefore you get a visual glitch. I placed the punching bag next to the door, then noticed whenever my zois goes to use the puching bag, the door doesn’t close behind him, which makes it look like he’s puching through the door haha A tough guy indeed! Screenshot to illustrate it is below: 20251030171035_1 2560×1440 395 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:**

1. The door will remain open for as long as it would realistically stay open when you open a door and stand in its way irl
2. If a Zoi is standing by it close enough, the door will automatically open and stay open for as they stand/interact there
3. Awesome for some Garage Bands (my Zoi on guitar and the singing Zoi are keeping the door open)!
