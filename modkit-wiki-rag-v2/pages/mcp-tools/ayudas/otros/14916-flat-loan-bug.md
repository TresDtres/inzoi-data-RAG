# Flat loan bug

**Sintoma:** Try to repay the loan interest via the SMS message in the phone. Whenever I get asked to repay the loan I took to buy the flat my ZOIs live in, I pay just the interest rate (as I can’t afford to pay the whole thing) and the amount due never gets lower. It stays at either 45,543 MEW or 45,093 MEW and never lowers. I think I already paid the interest rate at least 8 times if not more

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi, this isn’t a bug, it works this way, even though we’ve already expressed the desire for it to be changed. With each installment you only pay the interest portion, but not the principal portion, which can only be paid in full
