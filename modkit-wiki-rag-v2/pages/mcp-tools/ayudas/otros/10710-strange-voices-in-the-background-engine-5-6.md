# Strange voices in the background [engine 5.6]

**Sintoma:** When I play on multiple plots, I hear strange voices in the background, as if it’s news on TV or radio. The thing is, I don’t have the radio turned on at that time (I’m not even sure if there’s a radio news option?), and on one of the plots I don’t even have a TV. Once it starts, it keeps mumbling in the same place. And I don’t know if I have something turned on, or if it depends on the plot location, or if it’s a bug

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi! I don’t have the beta, so I can’t play to test it for several weeks. I’m not sure if your problem is similar to mine, but for me, when people use the computer, even when it’s off, it continues. I found out it was coming from the computer by searching and getting closer to the noise. I tested by putting the computer in my inventory, and the noise stopped. It was a laptop the first time it happened, and I thought it was the only one with this bug, but it’s the same with any computer. If you have a computer, you can try putting it in your inventory to test, otherwise, I have no idea ^^
