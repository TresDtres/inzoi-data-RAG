**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** How much Resort Coin (used at those Cahaya Resort Kiosks) does your Zoi currently have? If they have enough, but the options are still greyed out, it might be a bug. Try searching in Bug Reports to see if others have had the same issue.
