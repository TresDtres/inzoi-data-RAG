# Objects are not deletable

**Sintoma:** I removed a house and now I can’t remove these remaining objects. I tried everything, but they won’t go away! 1 1920×1080 766 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Over at the fish thread , @Yoshimou suggested Can't delete NPC's FISH ON AIR Draw a wall through the fish and they will be deleted. You can also use the same method and press undo, after the wall disappears and the fish appear again it makes them selectable again and can be deleted with the sledge hammer tool. Maybe this works here too? So you could draw a wall through the objects and see if they get deleted?
