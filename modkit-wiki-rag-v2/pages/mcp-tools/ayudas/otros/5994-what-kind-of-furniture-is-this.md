# What kind of furniture is this?

**Sintoma:** I have never seen such a piece of furniture, what is it? For me it looks upside down or unfinished(?) We can’t stack it, so what are these rods for? Or is there a surface missing? But we can place objects on it. grafik 1920×1080 687 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** A piece of glass goes on top so that there’s a space between the wooden box part and the glass shelf. Sometimes the box will have a door so that it can be used for storage. I’ve seen them with magazines on top of the box and then a vase or plant on the glass over it. I’m not saying that’s what this weirdness is with the legs in the air, just that I’ve seen similar
