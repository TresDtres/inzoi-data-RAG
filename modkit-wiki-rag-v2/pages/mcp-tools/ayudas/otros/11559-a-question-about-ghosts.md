# a question about ghosts

**Sintoma:** I have noticed our mortal zoi’s cannot interact much with the ghosts unless you use the bell but then sometimes the zoi’s ends up in a strange animation limbo. They walk around like they are drunk / delirious and that animation sticks and it doesn’t matter how many times i have used the emergancy reset button the animation is not giving in, they will never walk normally after that so i always find myself going back to a previous save to skip. I have even tested out with my test zoi to chat with the librarian and i have even allowed my test zoi to “camp” the library just to get a small hint of everything but nothing is happening. I have seen places being haunted this is why i have been ponder

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I don’t think this is a bug, it’s more likely a game error. My Zoe in several different saves would hold funerals for friends who had “passed away” and each time she would start acting strange, as if a spirit had possessed her (she became lethargic and could barely move, constantly stumbled over invisible objects, and kept yawning all the time). Only restarting the game saved me from this horror
