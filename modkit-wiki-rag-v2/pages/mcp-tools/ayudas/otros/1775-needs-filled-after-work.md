# Needs filled after work

**Sintoma:** I have a question and I am not sure, if it is a feature or a bug. After a working day, all the needs are filled up and I am not happy about that. Is this normal?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Yes, this is normal for work and school in the game. I believe it’s to keep Zoi from dying at work or having their vital needs interfere with the system. Other needs, like Fun and Social, are filled during the school/work day through specific tasks (e.g., “Make New Friends” at school). The way I think of it is that the Zoi is taking care of their needs on their own at their workplaces
