# [ 0.5.3 ] Zois autonomously cook themselves a group meal because they are hungry, but do not eat from it afterwards.

**Sintoma:** Balanced Painting SmallTalk fix Robin Rover’s Crazy Age Groups Windows 10 Professional / 22H2 / 19045.6691 13th Gen Intel(R) Core i9-13900KF 3.00 GHz NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Ti 192 Gb Ram, 4TB NVmE with 2.2tb left. Wait until a hungry zoi decides to cook a group meal. Basically, your ZOI gets hungry. He goes to fridge and cooks a group meal (disregarding the 25 leftover in the fridge, of course !) He completes the group meal, sets it on the table, then proceeds not to eat from it. Now, he MAY eat from it later, or decide to cook something else, or may decide to grab a leftover from the fridge. Sometimes you end-up with several meals on a table from that behavior. Please fix it so if they coo

**Causa:** baby’s crying Balanced Painting SmallTalk fix Robin Rover’s Crazy Age Groups Windows 10 Professional / 22H2 / 19045

**Solucion:** Hello @Samuriko , The issue has been addressed in the v0.7.0 update . [v0.7.0] Bug Fix List Reduced the frequency of cooking being cancelled due to a newborn’s crying The cause of this issue was the ‘newborn’s crying’ so we have adjusted the frequency of cooking being cancelled due to baby’s crying
