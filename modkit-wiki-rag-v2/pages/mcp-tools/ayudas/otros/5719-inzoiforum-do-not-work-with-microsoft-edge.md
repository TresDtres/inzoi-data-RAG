# INZOIFORUM do not work with Microsoft EDGE

**Sintoma:** On this website, INZOI FORUM, I can’t do anything when I log in with Windows Edge. The page appears gray, and no actions are possible. There are no problems with other browsers

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Found the cause, deleted the EDGE extension “ YTube AdSkipper” , and the problem is solved. YTube Ad Skipper prevented the cookie notification from appearing, and therefore access was denied
