# Can men marry each other now, and can men have children?

**Sintoma:** I’d like to ask everyone, I remember that The Sims 4 had the option for male pregnancy. Does Inzoi have this? My two male Zoi, can they have the ability to reproduce and let our children inherit their appearance, personality, and other traits?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hey there Yes, partners of any gender can now decide to either adopt OR have kids together via any computer in the game. The second option blends / mixes the DNA of both parent Zois together, like ordinarily conceived children would. The only downside of option 2 is, that only one of the parents gets listed as such. It doesn’t really affect gameplay much IMO, but I just wanted to let you know
