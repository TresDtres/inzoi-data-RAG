# Why are there no gay Zois in cities other than the city of origin?

**Sintoma:** There are no ‘gay’ Zois in cities other than the city of origin. I made a new playthrough after the October patch. I made a gay female young adult in Dowon. At first everything was fine she meet a few gals who were interested. But decided to go to Cahaya on vacation. While in Cahaya she could not find a single woman who were attracted to other women! I talked to at least a dozen, every single one.. straight. Went back to Dowon all was fine again. So I decided to check out Bliss Bay. I ‘interviewed about a half dozen female Zois. Every single one straight! Oh and yes I checked my Zois preferences

**Causa:** gender preference settings after city travel There are no ‘gay’ Zois in cities other than the city of origin

**Solucion:** Hello @ulteriormodem , This issue has been fixed through v0.5.0 update. Fixed an issue where romance interactions were unavailable in the destination city due to gender preference settings after city travel
