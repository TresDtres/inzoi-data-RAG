# Stove & Counter Gets Dirty Every Time Zoi Cooks

**Sintoma:** Cook something on the stove Not sure if this is a bug or intentional. Either way, I feel this needs to be tuned because, while it may be realistic to clean your stove/counters every time after meal prep in real life, it’s a nuisance in the game since Zois have to cook most meals individually. My Zois keep complaining about using dirty furniture when cooking multiple things in a row, and having to clean between every dish preparation is too much. Happens with all stove types and counters where food is prepared Date: Started occurring either with the August 2025 update or one of its hotfixes. Dirty Countertop stove 1920×1080 261 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** This issue no longer occurs after an update adjusted house-cleanliness frequency!
