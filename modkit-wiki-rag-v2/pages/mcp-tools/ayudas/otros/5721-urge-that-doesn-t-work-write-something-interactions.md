# Urge that doesn’t work (“Write something” interactions)

**Sintoma:** Zoi wants to “write down today’s thoughts in the diary.” The interaction doesn’t work (the Urge doesn’t disappear), Zoi takes the diary in her hand for a moment and puts it back. I’ve tried all the “Write something” options, and none of them work. I’m attaching a video. drive.google.com inZOI 2025-10-02 00-33-54.mp4 Google Drive file

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi, thanks for your help and the idea you gave me. I tried another bookcase and it didn’t work. Then I inventoryed the desk and found this “pile of books” underneath that I hadn’t noticed. I removed them and put the desk back in and now it works. Screenshot 2025-10-03 024548 497×408 206 KB
