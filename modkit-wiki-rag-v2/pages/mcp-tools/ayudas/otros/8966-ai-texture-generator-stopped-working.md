# AI texture generator STOPPED working

**Sintoma:** ) Win 11, Ryzen 9 7900X 12-core, GeForce RTX 4070 SUPER, 32 GB RAM Hello everyone! This issue occurred to me a long time ago, maybe even before the August update , but I am not quite sure. There is an AI texture generator for the game, which you can use for clothes and furniture. After I got the game at launch I used it pretty often, it was working fine . But then after a few months it stopped working completely. I can upload my own textures and I can download stuff from Canvas but I can’t use the AI generator itself . If I write anything, a long prompt or just a word, the “loading wheel” just spins and nothing happens. There are even times when the game just freezes and I have to force-clos

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hallelujah, I found the problem! Reshade was somehow blocking the process, uninstalling it fixed it ! Which is interesting, because I clearly remember installing it after the AI problem occurred, so that’s why I didn’t even think that it could be the culprit. (I didn’t update Reshade for a while now, maybe the newest version doesn’t have this problem) Needless to say, I am happy it works again (although I can’t use my favourite preset )
