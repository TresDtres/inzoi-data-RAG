# Steak is broken

**Sintoma:** When cooking the steak, after it is finished, my zoi puts it on a table and does not eat from it automatically, he proceeds to make himself something different to eat instead. The steak cannot be used to call others to have a group meal. The steak cannot be picked up to be put into the fridge (“you cannot use that”) but you can put the steak into your pocket inventory. PC, Steam, no mods, GPU 3080TI, Win 10

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I think I found the problem. It was the type of fridge I used (the fridge with the integrated ice cube machine for 3200 bucks) - when I switched to another fridge, I never had the trouble with not being able to put in food anymore
