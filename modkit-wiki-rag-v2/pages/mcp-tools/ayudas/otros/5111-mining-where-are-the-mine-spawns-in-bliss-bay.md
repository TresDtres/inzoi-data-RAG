# Mining… Where are the mine spawns in Bliss Bay?

**Sintoma:** Bliss Bay Clover Park has no mine spawns. Compared to Dowon and Cahaya parks which have a ton, I’ve only ever found two in Clover park and one is a regular mine spot (called mining spot) and the other is silver mining spot. Where are the mine spots in Bliss Bay? I rarely play in Bliss Bay and this just makes me want to play with this world even less

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I found a lot of mining spots around the School in Bliss Bay. Not on the school property itself, but next to the sidewalk around the school, in the bushes, etc. Hope this helps!
