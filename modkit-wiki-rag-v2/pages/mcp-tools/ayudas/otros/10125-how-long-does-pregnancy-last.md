# How long does pregnancy last?

**Sintoma:** Anyone know how many days a Zoi is pregnant for? I thought hovering over the pregnancy icon would show it but it doesn’t. Screenshot 2026-02-14 214449 721×320 48.6 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Had to google myself ^^’ Here’s what I found: In inZOI , a standard pregnancy lasts exactly three in-game days , divided into early, middle, and late stages. After these three days, players must click a red exclamation mark icon on the Zoi to trigger the birth, or it will occur automatically about five hours later. Key Details About Pregnancy: Duration: 3 days (in-game time). Birth Trigger: Manual (via UI icon) or automatic (~5 hours after). Restrictions: The Zoi’s appearance cannot be edited during pregnancy. Symptoms: Includes mood swings (sad attitude), rapid hunger, and, in some cases, bugs that might delay the birth. Preparation: It is advised to prepare a nursery and have funds available
