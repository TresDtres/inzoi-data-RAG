# baking can’t complete

**Sintoma:** when my zoi tries to bake, she proceeds to cook as normal as can be seen here: Screenshot 2026-07-10 190507 1756×1018 481 KB however, once she’s done mixing, the bowl dissappears and the interaction gets cancelled Screenshot 2026-07-10 190519 1907×981 497 KB same result happens when clikicking bake or restck items for sale. zoi is only able to bake cake normally. cookies, muffins, and doughnuts glitch out for both level 0 and level 1 recipes (could be more). cooking recipes such as pancakes seem to work as intended

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:**

1. I had this bug once a long time ago and it turns out, when something else is close to the surface (like your coffee grinder), it glitches into the counter and one cannot continue to bake/prepare
2. Either move the grinder somewhere else entirely or try baking on a different counter, that is completely free
