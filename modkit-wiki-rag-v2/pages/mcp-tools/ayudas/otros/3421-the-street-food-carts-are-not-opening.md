# The street food carts are not opening.

**Sintoma:** I’m playing in Chahaya Village, but at some point, the food carts stopped opening. They remain in a closed state, and even resetting them through editing doesn’t work. I also tried hiring and assigning staff, but it still doesn’t work

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi everyone ( @all )! Thanks to your reports, the following issue has been resolved with v0.3.4 Fixed an issue where some street vendor NPCs in Cahaya would not appear after the season changed → For save files where the issue had already occurred, NPCs will resume going to work within up to 7 in-game days. However, the following issue is still under investigation and remains on the Known Issues list: Some street vendor NPCs in Dowon and Bliss Bay may intermittently fail to show up for work during gameplay. (8/28) We’ll provide an update here as soon as we have more information. Thank you
