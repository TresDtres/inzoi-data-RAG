# ‘Underside’ Texture on Floating Stairs Visually Resets After Loading.(Update)

**Sintoma:** I’m experiencing a visual bug with the textures on the underside of the stairs . As you can see in the attached photo, I applied a brown texture to the stair underside. However, the texture doesn’t appear visually; it just looks blank or untextured. If I re-apply the color, it looks correct. But as soon as I reload the game (or log back in), the texture resets to default and disappears again. This happens every time I restart the game. Hope this helps and can be fixed in a future patch! 2456740_20251105041716_1 1920×1080 510 KB 2456740_20251101155934_1 1920×1080 214 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:**

1. When customizing stair treads, the customization may not apply to the stair landing
2. When continuing from a save file, customizations applied to stair walls may be reset
