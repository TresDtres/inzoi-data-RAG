# Catching a cold happens too early and too often

**Sintoma:** It’s raining and Vlad just went to take the bus and caught a cold, it happened also earlier to two other Zois of the same household just a few hours ago. That’s unrealistic and shouldn’t happen that frequently. image 1920×1080 257 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi everyone ( @here ), We’ve adjusted the conditions for catching a cold when being outside in the rain without an umbrella with the v0.6.0 update . Previously, Zois could catch a cold after 1 hour in the rain. This has now been extended to 6 hours, making colds much less frequent in normal gameplay. Thank you all for your feedback!
