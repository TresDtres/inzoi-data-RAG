# How should I get Zoi to drive on her own?

**Sintoma:** I don’t know how to make Zoi drive vehicles on their own around the city. Even though I turned off manual driving in the settings, if I want them to drive, I still need manual control. Otherwise, they can only walk and use public transportation. What should I do, or is this a bug? I hope everyone can help me out. Thanks!

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** If you have a car and want the Zoi to autonomously drive somewhere: go to the map view select the place you want to go select “drive here” Then your Zoi should go to the street, get his car, sit into the car and drive to the location you selected autonomously. Same for the scheduled events for example. When the notification pops up there should be an option “go to vehicle” and not only “go there” if you own a car. If you select the “go to vehicle” option your Zoi would go to get his car and drive there instead of taking the bus or the subway
