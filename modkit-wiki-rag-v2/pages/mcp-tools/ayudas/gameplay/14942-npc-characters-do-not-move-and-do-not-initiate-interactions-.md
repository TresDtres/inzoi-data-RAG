# NPC characters do not move and do not initiate interactions on their own.

**Sintoma:** Could you please help? Nothing I’ve tried has fixed this issue. When I go to the high school, all the young Zois (NPCs) stand motionless in various parts of the building throughout the school day—they don’t interact, walk around, or talk to one another. They only leave the school en masse once the day is over. Essentially, the only time they move of their own accord is when school hours end; they don’t leave their spots even during scheduled activities like classes or breaks. They don’t attend lessons or eat. This problem isn’t limited to the high school; it happens at workplaces too. My young Zoi works at the grocery store, and when she arrives, dozens of NPC Zois are standing right in fron

**Causa:** a broken property or schedule update

**Solucion:**

1. If not, I am afraid someone from the team needs to take a look at it or some other member has an idea Often, broken code fragments remain in your system cache after an update, causing AI pathfinding to loop or freeze
2. Close the game completely
3. Open your Steam library, right-click on inZOI , and select Properties
4. Go to the Installed Files tab and click Verify integrity of game files
5. Head to your local app data folder (usually C:\Users\[Username]\AppData\Local\inZOI or Krafton ) and safely delete the Saved\Crashes or temporary cache folders if they exist
6. OR Test a “Clean” New Save File If the issue is hard-baked into the specific 20260705_AutoSave file attached to that thread, the save data itself might be corrupted due to a broken property or schedule update
7. Go straight to the grocery store or high school to see if the NPCs move normally
