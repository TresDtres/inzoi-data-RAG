# What is the difference between “paint a picture” and “paint with soul”?

**Sintoma:** What exactly is the difference? Is there any? My Zoi doesn’t have any special artistic traits that might influence that. Both let me choose which type of painting style I want and both raise the art skill level. image_2026-07-03_191537457 691×613 78.2 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Unlike the regular “Paint” option “Paint for Soul” focuses more on relaxation and self expression. Its primary purpose is not to make money but to improve your Zoi’s emotional well-being. If your Zoi is feeling stressed, sad, overwhelmed, or simply needs inspiration “Paint for Soul” is usually the better choice
