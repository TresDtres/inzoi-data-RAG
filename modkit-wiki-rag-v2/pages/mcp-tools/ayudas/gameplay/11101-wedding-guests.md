# Wedding guests

**Sintoma:** I’m trying weddings for the first time and I have a distinction between a wedding participant and a wedding guest. In Polish, these are the same thing. What’s the difference between these functions and why do they have different clothing categories programmed by default?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I’ve played weddings in the game in English translation. The Officiator is the one who will read the speech while the couple stand before the speech podium and one of them has the bouquet of flowers. (It can be the wrong one sometimes, so I don’t know what determines who holds the bouquet). Anyhow, “Wedding Party Member” is what it’s called in English, and in many Western cultures, the Wedding Party are your Best Man and/or Groomsmen, and also Maid of Honour and/or Bridesmaids. They’re part of the Wedding Party because they stand with the bride and groom during the ceremony. The difference between Wedding Party Member and Wedding Guest is that some couples choose to have a colour scheme for their wedding party. For example, the bride might have her best female friend(s) wear soft pink so they match. The groom might have his best male friend(s) wear light blue. Okay, lame colour choices there. But I hope you understand the example. The wedding celebration event in inZOI isn’t very complex right now, but they might add more for each of these different groups in the future
