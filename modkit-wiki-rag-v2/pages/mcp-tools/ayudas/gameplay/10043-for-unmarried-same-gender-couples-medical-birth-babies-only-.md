# For unmarried same gender couples, medical birth babies only consider one parent as family

**Sintoma:** When a Zoi chooses Medical Birth from the computer or the laptop and gets consent from a Zoi of the same gender whom they are not married to, the baby only sees the Zoi who applied for the Medical Birth as family. The baby does not recognize the second parent who has given consent as family. Please fix this. Again, this is only an issue for unmarried same gender couples

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi @Nyami , Thank you for your report. Currently, when a Zoi applies for Medical Birth without being married, the system recognizes only the applicant as the legal parent. This applies to both same-gender and opposite-gender couples . In other words, if the couple is not married, the consenting partner is not automatically registered as a parent. This is the current design specification of the system. We understand that this may not align with all family play styles, and we appreciate your understanding. Since this is not a bug, I will close the thread. However, you can always mention this in the Wishlist category
