# Zoi Marriage Issue

**Sintoma:** 64 bit, 16 GB. 15.7 GB usable. After moving to Cayana (this could have been previously without me noticing) My zois attraction were set to “none” and they both became single, although newly weds. Also, Zois relationship status became “seeking no one.” They cannot romantically interact with one another. They even get denied by everyone based on their relationship/attraction issue. They used to be attracted to the opposite gender and wanted relationships. How can I fix this? They even have a child together

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Just click on your ZOI, Style Yourself (like you would do to modify hair or clothes) and on the left, near the name and gender, there’s an icon where you can choose their gender and attracted to settings. This at least will make them interested in romance again, but not with their spouse
