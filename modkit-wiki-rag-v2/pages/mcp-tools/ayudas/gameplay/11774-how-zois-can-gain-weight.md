# How zois can gain weight?

**Sintoma:** My zois do not gain weight even when eating junk food. Is this a bug? Or am I doing smth wrong? How can I make my zois plus size without using donuts?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** I think you have to have your Zois keep eating when their Hunger bar is full for them to gain weight. It used to happen really easily in earlier versions of the game, but I think that’s been changed. You may have to do it several times over
