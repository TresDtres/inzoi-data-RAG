# Has anyone ever opened the ‘Plain Treasure Chest’ - on a Wednesday?

**Sintoma:** I wonder what’s in it? And why it can only be opened on a Wednesday ö.Ö’ Are there others of these chests anywhere? It’s Sunday in my game rn and until Wednesday, I’ve forgotten about it ^^’ I could set an ingame event but I’m probably with another household then. grafik 1920×1080 674 KB

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Yes, it’s part of the resort treasure hunt which is only active on Wednesdays. You can enter it by purchasing a ticket at one of the kiosks in the spa. There are several of these chests on the resort island (plus some special ones with different rewards) and each one gives you a small gold bar which is worthless in itself, but once you have ten of them, you can exchange them for resort coins at the spa kiosk. You can then use the resort coins to purchase rewards. Be warned, however: the total resort coins gained from exchanging the 10 gold bars is the same amount it takes to enter the treasure hunt in the first place (or at least it was when I played). I think this must be a mistake, but as far as I know it hasn’t been changed yet, so the only things you gain overall are the extra rewards from the ‘special’ chests you find. IMO it’s not worth it given the amount of time I had to spend chasing down all the chests
