# remove ghosts

**Sintoma:** hey how do i remove a ghost from my household shes so annoying and occuping everything i tried ordering this soulguide guy and asked to let the ghost rest or something but he just rang a bell and nothing happened and theres no cheats that completely deletes zois on the city map there is also no decreased zois shown even tho she is dead and a ghost pls help

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hello! I had this problem yesterday. After I downloaded a house from canvas, 3 ghosts appeared on the lot. I could be wrong, but I think the ghosts came with the house lol. So here’s what I did to get rid of them. Go in cheat mode (Control + Shift + C)/ make sure you have cheats enabled already Type in this cheat: showzoiinfo true (displays Zoi IDs and coordinates above zois’ heads) After you gather the ghost’s ID, then enter this cheat code removezoi [zoi ID] (where it states zoi ID you will replace that part with the ghost ID.) After this, the ghost should ‘die again’ and will be gone/removed from your lot. Afterwards, type this cheat showzoiinfo false (turns off the display of Zoi IDs and coordinates above zois’ heads) Here’s an example of the cheats used together without the explanation: showzoiinfo true removezoi 1556 showzoiinfo false Hopes this helps!!!
