# My zois can’t lose weight…

**Sintoma:** If I make them exercise, all I get is them getting muscular right away, but there’s no way they’ll lose weight again. With the Male Zois, well… But some Female Zois end up too muscular, they look like they’re from bodybuilding… There’s no middle ground, or I don’t know how to do it…

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Also as a tip in case you don’t want to disable weight gain, you can buy a Diet Donut from the Meow Store and your zoi will go back to their weight!
