# My ambition wealth level isn’t being met [beta engine 5.6]

**Sintoma:** After selecting the wealth aspiration, the level “buy furniture for 800 meow 5 x” is not being counted for me despite purchasing a furniture set for the required amount. This happened before with another family when I was playing on the base engine. However, the level requiring me to have a specific amount of money in the account is automatically counted. Does it have to be a single object for 800? I tried a furniture set at that amount, even more, and it doesn’t register

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Yes, each individual purchase must be 800 meow, so 800x5. That’s how it worked for me, even though I haven’t done it recently
