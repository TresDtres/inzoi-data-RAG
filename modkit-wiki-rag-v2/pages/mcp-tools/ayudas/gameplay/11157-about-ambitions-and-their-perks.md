# About Ambitions and their perks

**Sintoma:** Does anyone know if you complete an Ambition and start another one, will you lose the earned perk? Is it possible to earn more than one perk by completing ambitions?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** You keep whatever you achieved by completing an ambition. You can switch between ambitions throughout the game play and complete them incrementally
