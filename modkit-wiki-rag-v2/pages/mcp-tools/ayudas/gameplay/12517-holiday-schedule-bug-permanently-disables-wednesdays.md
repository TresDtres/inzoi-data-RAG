# Holiday Schedule Bug Permanently Disables Wednesdays

A holiday triggered correctly, and all of my Zois took the day off. However, ever since then, every Wednesday has been completely blank on every Zoi’s schedule , even though they are still technically employed and should have work that day. I confirmed this across multiple households/Zois. Current Behavior Wednesdays are permanently disabled/blank Work still exists in the schedule preview Wednesday cannot be toggled back on “Turn On A

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hello everyone ( @here ), We’d like to clear up some confusion about certain days being disabled. Some days have their schedule intentionally turned off because they fall on a holiday, so they can’t be edited (such as the Spring Festival, Halloween, and so on). We were aware that these disabled days could look like a bug, so we’ve made an improvement: a Holiday icon now appears next to the toggle (see the image below). image 306×184 13.2 KB Since this isn’t a bug, we’ll be closing this thread.
