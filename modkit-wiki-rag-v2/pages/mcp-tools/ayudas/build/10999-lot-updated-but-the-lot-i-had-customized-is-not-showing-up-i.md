# Lot updated but the lot I had customized is not showing up in building presets that I am aware of.

**Sintoma:** I am happy to see all the new updates that Inzoi creators have provided and look forward to trying everything out. If I understood correctly, lots that are occupied by Zois but are required to be updated will result in the lot being saved under Presets in build mode, but I am not seeing my customized lot there. The update also brought the household funds from over 15,000 to a few hundred dollars. I am wondering if anyone has any suggestions on how if possible I can restore the lot that I had previously customized. Also, is there a way to get the money back?

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Go into Build Mode Check the auto-save box Click on your property, will be the generic white one with a money amount Re-place it in your lot If you do not have enough money, you can click delete all to get a refund if another property was resert in lieu of yours. image 223×163 8.38 KB
