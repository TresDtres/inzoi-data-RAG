# Staircases with a stair wall are no longer hollow

**Sintoma:** This may be a bug or simply a design decision by the team to change stair walls but hollowed staircases allowed for more building options by being able to place objects under it. In some countries it is very common for staircases to have a closet or small bathroom under them ( e.g: Harry Potter slept under the stairs ). Please undo this change or allow for users the option to fully enclose staircases or hollow staircases when stairwall is enabled. Graphics: GeForce Nvidia 3070 Ti (24GB) Processor: AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D 16-Core Processor Enter build mode and add a staircase. Enter customization mode and enable the stair wall for the staircase. You will see it is no longer hollow. Screenshot 1

**Causa:** desconocida

**Solucion:** Hi @Luna_H , Thank you for bringing this to our attention. As you mentioned, this isn’t a bug — the recent update fixed the issue where gaps appeared between stair walls.
